Try rice, basmati, coconut oil, or a partial spelling such as ric.

This site makes the product quality requirements in FSSAI's public Food-'O'-Copoeia easier to search. Entries include their product category, FSSR limit, test method, and a link back to the source.

This is an unofficial independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FSSAI. Food-'O'-Copoeia is a reference, not a legal document; original regulations and Gazette publications prevail. Source, downloads, and methodology.
